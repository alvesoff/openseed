Questions that always come up

How much does custom software cost?

It depends on the scope, which is why the quote is fixed before we start. You describe the problem, we come back within 3 business days with what can be built, how long it takes and how much it costs. An MVP usually runs 4 to 8 weeks.

How long until the system is ready?

An MVP takes 4 to 8 weeks. Internal systems vary with the number of workflows, and delivery happens in one week cycles, with a test environment open to you from the start.

Who owns the code after the project ends?

The system is yours to use however you want, for as long as you want, with nothing more to pay for that. The source code stays with OpenSeed, who maintains and evolves it. Your data and your domain are yours. If your case requires the source code in your hands, say so before closing: it changes the shape and the price of the proposal.

Do you work outside Ribeirão Preto?

Yes. Our base is Ribeirão Preto, Brazil, and in person coverage includes Sertãozinho, Cravinhos and Jardinópolis. Projects elsewhere are handled remotely, with the same follow up meetings.

What does AI at the core actually mean?

It means applying AI where it removes manual work: an agent that queries the system and resolves a request, automatic reading of invoices and contracts, plain language search over company data. When a simple script is the better answer, we say so in the proposal.
